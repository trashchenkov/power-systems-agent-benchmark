**Method**
- **Baseline customer-minutes/year** = Σ (annual_fault_rate × customers_interrupted × base_restoration_minutes) = **4024**

- **SAIDI** = customer-minutes / 320 customers
- **CAIDI** = customer-minutes / 45.8 interruptions
