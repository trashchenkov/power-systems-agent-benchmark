The commercial PV LCOE benchmark is done. `submission.json` is in the packet directory.

**Results (USD/MWh):**
- Scenarios: s_low 61.90, s_base 64.55, s_perm 66.46, s_inter 67.37, s_high 69.82
- Quantiles: p10 62.96, p50 66.46, p90 68.84
- Mean across scenarios: 66.02

Values follow the `calculation_spec`: CAPEX from hard + soft costs per scenario, annual cost via CRF and fixed O&M, LCOE divided by 3,200 MWh/year. Quantiles use linear interpolation on the five scenario LCOE values.
