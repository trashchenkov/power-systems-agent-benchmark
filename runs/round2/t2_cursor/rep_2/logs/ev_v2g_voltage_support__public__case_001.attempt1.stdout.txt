**Method**
- Required ΔV per bus: `target_voltage_pu − base_voltage_pu` → bus_2: 0.036 pu, bus_3: 0.028 pu.
- Unclipped Q: ΔV / `q_sensitivity_pu_per_kvar` → 22.5 kvar (bus_2), 23.33… kvar (bus_3).
- Per-bus Q limit: Σ √(S² − P²) for EVSEs on that bus → ~25.36 kvar (bus_2), ~13.856 kvar (bus_3).
- Dispatch: min(needed, capability) → bus_2 hits target (0.98 pu); bus_3 is capability-limited.
- `total_abs_q_kvar`: 36.356… (sum of |Q| per bus).
