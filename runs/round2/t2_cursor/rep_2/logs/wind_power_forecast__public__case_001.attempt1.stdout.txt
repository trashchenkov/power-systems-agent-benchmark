**Method**
- Power curve on density-corrected speed \(v_c = v \cdot (\rho/\rho_{ref})^{1/3}\) with cut-in 3 m/s, rated 12 m/s, cut-out 25 m/s, farm size 8×2.5 MW = 20 MW.
- **Bias** = mean historical residual (actual − curve estimate) over h-4…h-1 → **0.024847 MW**.
- Each step: `clip(curve_estimate + bias, 0, 20)`.
