**Method**

- Minimum case: R + fault resistance 0.005 pu → |Z_min| ≈ 0.2636 pu  
- I_base = 100 / (√3 × 10.5) kA  
- Ik_max = 1.1 / |Z_bolted| × I_base, Sk_max = √3 × 10.5 × Ik_max  
- Ik_min = 1.0 / |Z_min| × I_base
