**Approach**
- Power balance each hour: `diesel + (pv − curtailment) + battery_discharge − battery_charge = load` (no shedding).
- SOC with efficiencies: `Δsoc = charge_mw × η_ch × Δt − discharge_mw / η_dis × Δt`.
- Battery power on **2 MW** steps (−8 … 12 MW); diesel within **15–70 MW**; PV curtailment only when needed for diesel limits.
- Exhaustive search over discrete battery schedules (11³ combinations), minimum diesel variable cost per feasible schedule.
