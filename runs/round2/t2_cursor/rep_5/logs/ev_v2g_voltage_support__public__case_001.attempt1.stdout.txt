Wrote `submission.json` with the EV voltage-support solution.

**Method**
- Per bus: `q_needed = (target_voltage_pu − base_voltage_pu) / q_sensitivity_pu_per_kvar`
- Per EV: `q_max = √(s_rating_kva² − active_power_kw²)`; summed by bus
- `q_dispatch = min(q_needed, aggregate_q_cap)` (bus_2 fully reaches 0.98 pu; bus_3 is capability-limited)
- `corrected_voltage_pu = base_voltage_pu + q_dispatch × sensitivity`
- `total_abs_q_kvar` = sum of |bus Q|
