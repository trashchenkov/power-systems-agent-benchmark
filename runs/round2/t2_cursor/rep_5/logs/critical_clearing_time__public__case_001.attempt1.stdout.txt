Solved the SMIB equal-area critical clearing time case from `task.json` and wrote `submission.json`.

**Inputs:** Pm = 0.9 pu, prefault Pmax = 2.0, post-fault Pmax = 1.5, H = 4 s, f₀ = 50 Hz (zero transfer during the fault).

**Formulas used:**
- δ₀ = asin(Pm / prefault_pmax)
- δ_max = π − asin(Pm / postfault_pmax)
- cos(δ_cr) = (Pm·(δ_max − δ₀) + postfault_pmax·cos(δ_max)) / postfault_pmax
- t_cr = √(4H·(δ_cr − δ₀) / (2π·f₀·Pm))
