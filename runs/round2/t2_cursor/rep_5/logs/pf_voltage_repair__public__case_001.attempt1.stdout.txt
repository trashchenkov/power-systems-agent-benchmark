**Problem:** Bus 2 is at 0.925 pu (below the 0.95 pu minimum). Bus 1 is already fine at 1.0 pu.

**Model:** Adding capacitor reactive power at bus 2 raises its voltage by `0.006` pu per Mvar (`q_cap_mvar`).

**Minimum-cost fix:**  
`q_cap = (0.95 − 0.925) / 0.006 = 25/6 ≈ 4.167` Mvar → final voltage 0.95 pu, cost 4.167.

Only `bus_2` has a defined sensitivity/action, so the submission applies compensation there and leaves other buses unchanged.
