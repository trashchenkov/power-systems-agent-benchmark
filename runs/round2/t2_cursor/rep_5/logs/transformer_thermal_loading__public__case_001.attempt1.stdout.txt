Solved the IEC 60076-7 exponential thermal loading case and wrote `submission.json`.

**Method:** For each load step, steady-state top-oil rise is `55 × ((1 + 5K²)/6)^0.8`; oil rise evolves from the previous step with τ = 3 h; top-oil °C = ambient + rise; hot-spot = top-oil + `23 × K^1.3`; ageing rate = `2^((θ_hs − 98)/6)`; total ageing = Σ(rate × duration).
