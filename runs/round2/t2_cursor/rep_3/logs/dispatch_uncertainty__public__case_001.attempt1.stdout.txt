`submission.json` is in the packet directory with the least-cost feasible dispatch.

**Method**
- For each period, worst-case net load is the max of `load_mw − renewable_mw` over `normal`, `high_load`, and `low_renewable` → t1: 72, t2: 77, t3: 69 MW.
- Required thermal output = that value + 8 MW reserve → 80, 85, and 77 MW.
- Dispatch minimizes variable cost (g1 at $18/MWh before g2 at $36/MWh) while respecting pmin/pmax.
