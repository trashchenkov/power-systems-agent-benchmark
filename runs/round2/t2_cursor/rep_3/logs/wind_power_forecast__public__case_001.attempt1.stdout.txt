**Method**
- **Power curve:** \(v_c = v \cdot (\rho/\rho_{\mathrm{ref}})^{1/3}\); cubic region between cut-in (3 m/s) and rated (12 m/s); full farm output (8×2.5 = **20 MW**) at rated and above; zero below cut-in or at/above cut-out (25 m/s).
- **Bias:** mean historical residual `actual_power_mw − power_curve_mw` over h-4…h-1 → **≈ 0.0248 MW**.
- **Forecasts:** `clip(curve + bias, 0, 20)` for t01–t04.
