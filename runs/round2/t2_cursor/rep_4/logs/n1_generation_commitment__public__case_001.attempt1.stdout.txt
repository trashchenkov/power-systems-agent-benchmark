**Problem:** Pick the cheapest committed set that covers 260 MW demand plus 40 MW reserve (300 MW total), and after losing the largest committed unit still has at least 260 MW (`n1_secure_capacity_mw`).

**Solution:** Commit **g1, g2, g3** (cost $1,950 = startup + no-load).

| Field | Value |
|--------|--------|
| Total capacity | 460 MW |
| After N-1 (loss of g3, 180 MW) | 280 MW ≥ 260 MW |
| Reserve (460 − 260) | 200 MW ≥ 40 MW |
