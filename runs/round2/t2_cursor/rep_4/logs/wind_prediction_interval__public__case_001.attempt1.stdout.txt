`submission.json` is in the packet directory with the wind-power prediction intervals computed from `task.json`.

**Method (per task spec):**
- **Corrected wind speed:** \(v_c = v \cdot (\rho / 1.225)^{1/3}\)
- **Farm power:** 8 × 2.5 MW turbines using cut-in 3 m/s, rated 12 m/s, cut-out 25 m/s and cubic region between cut-in and rated
- **Quantiles:** scenario powers sorted; linear interpolation at positions \(q \cdot (n-1)\) for p10, p50, p90
- **`average_interval_width_mw`:** mean of (p90 − p10) over t01–t03 → **7.5794402551359115 MW**
