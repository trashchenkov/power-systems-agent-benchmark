Wrote `submission.json` using the linear sensitivity model from `task.json`.

**Method**
- Target Q per bus: `(0.98 − V_base) / sensitivity`
- Per-bus capability: Σ √(S_rating² − P²) for EVSEs on that bus
- Dispatch: needed Q clipped to capability (bus_3 is limited; bus_2 is not)
- Corrected voltage: `V_base + sensitivity × Q_dispatch`
- `total_abs_q_kvar`: sum of |Q| over buses
