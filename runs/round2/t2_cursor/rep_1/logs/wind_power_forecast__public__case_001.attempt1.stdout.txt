**Method**
1. **Power curve** — Density-adjusted speed \(v_c = v \cdot (\rho/\rho_{ref})^{1/3}\), then cubic segment between cut-in (3 m/s) and rated (12 m/s), full farm output (8 × 2.5 = 20 MW) at rated+, zero below cut-in or at/above cut-out (25 m/s).
2. **Bias** — Mean of `(actual_power_mw − curve estimate)` over the four history hours → **≈ 0.0248 MW**.
3. **Forecasts** — `clip(curve + bias, 0, 20)` for t01–t04.
