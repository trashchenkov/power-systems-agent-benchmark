Solved the **N-1 generation commitment** case from `task.json` and wrote `submission.json`.

**Feasibility**
- **Demand + reserve:** 260 + 40 = **300 MW** → committed capacity must be ≥ 300 MW.
- **Reserve (reported):** available capacity − demand ≥ 40 MW.
- **N-1:** after losing the largest committed unit, remaining capacity must still cover **demand** (260 MW).

**Search**
Among all subsets, only **g1 + g2 + g3** (and the costlier full set) satisfy capacity and N-1. **g1, g2, g3** is least cost:

| Field | Value |
|--------|--------|
| Available capacity | 120 + 160 + 180 = **460 MW** |
| Reserve | 460 − 260 = **200 MW** |
| N-1 secure (loss of g3) | 460 − 180 = **280 MW** ≥ 260 |
| Commitment cost | (400+80) + (550+100) + (700+120) = **1950 USD** |
