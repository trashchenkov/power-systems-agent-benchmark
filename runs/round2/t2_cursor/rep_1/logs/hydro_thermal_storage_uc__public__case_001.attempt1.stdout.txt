Task solved and written to `submission.json`.

**Problem setup**
- Residual thermal need each hour: h1 75 MW, h2 90 MW, h3 85 MW (load minus fixed hydro and storage).
- Coal: 40–100 MW when on, \$22/MWh, 0.75 t/MWh; gas: 20–80 MW when on, \$38/MWh, 0.35 t/MWh.
- Total emissions cap: 200 t.

**Solution**
- Dispatch coal only at 75, 90, and 85 MW (within limits; gas is more expensive per MWh).
- **Total cost:** \$5,500 (250 MWh × \$22).
- **Emissions:** 187.5 t (250 MWh × 0.75), under the cap.
- **`emission_cap_satisfied`:** `true`.
