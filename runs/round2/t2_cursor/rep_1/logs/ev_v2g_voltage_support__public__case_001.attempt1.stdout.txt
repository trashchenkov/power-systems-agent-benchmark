Completed the EV/V2G voltage support calculation and wrote `submission.json`.

**Method**
- Target voltage: **0.98 pu**
- Per bus: `q_needed = (target − base_voltage) / q_sensitivity`
- Cap per bus: sum of `√(S_rating² − P²)` for all EVSEs on that bus
- Dispatch: `min(q_needed, q_cap)` (bus_2 reaches target; bus_3 is capability-limited)
- Corrected voltage: `base + sensitivity × q_dispatch`
- `total_abs_q_kvar`: sum of `|q_dispatch|` per bus

**Results**
| Bus   | Q dispatch (kvar) | Corrected V (pu) |
|-------|-------------------|------------------|
| bus_2 | 22.5              | 0.98             |
| bus_3 | ~13.856           | ~0.9686          |

**Total |Q|:** ~36.356 kvar
