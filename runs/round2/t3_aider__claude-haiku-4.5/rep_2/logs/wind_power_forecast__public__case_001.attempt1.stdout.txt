**Power curve formula:**

- v_c = wind_speed_m_s * (air_density_kg_m3 / 1.225)^(1/3)

- If v_c < 3.0 or v_c ≥ 25.0: power = 0

- If v_c ≥ 12.0: power = 8 * 2.5 = 20 MW

- Otherwise: power = 20 * ((v_c - 3.0) / (12.0 - 3.0))^3
