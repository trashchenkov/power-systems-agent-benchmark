## Analysis



The task requires computing the real-time ampacity of an overhead line using a 
steady-state heat-balance model. The key formula is:



**Ampacity = sqrt(max(convection + radiation - solar_gain, 0) / R_hot)**



Where:

- **R_hot** = (resistance_ohm_per_km_at_20c/1000) × (1 + 
resistance_temp_coeff_per_c × (T_max - 20))

- **convection** = convective_w_per_m_c_at_1ms × max(wind_speed_m_s, 0.05)^0.6 ×
(T_max - ambient_temperature_c)

- **radiation** = emissivity × 5.670374419e-8 × (π × diameter_mm/1000) × 
((T_max+273.15)^4 - (ambient+273.15)^4)

- **solar_gain** = solar_absorptivity × solar_w_per_m2 × (diameter_mm/1000)
