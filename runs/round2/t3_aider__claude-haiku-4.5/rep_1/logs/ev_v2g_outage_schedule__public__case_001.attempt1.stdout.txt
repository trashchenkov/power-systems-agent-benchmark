- 3 time periods (t1, t2, t3), each 0.5 hours

- Critical loads: t1=10kW, t2=12kW, t3=9kW

charge max

- EV_B: 32kWh capacity, 20kWh initial, 12kWh reserve, 6kW discharge max, 3kW 
charge max



**Strategy:**

1. For each period, maximize discharge to meet critical load

2. Respect reserve SOC (cannot go below it)

3. Account for efficiency (discharge at 0.9, charge at 0.95)

4. Respect power limits and dispatch steps
