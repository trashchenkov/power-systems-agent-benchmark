- Iteratively removing the worst measurement and re-estimating until no 
residuals are above a threshold.
- Reporting the identified bad measurements and the final, corrected state 
estimate.

**Step 3: The Iterative LNR Algorithm.**
I need to loop:
1.  **Estimate State:** Solve the WLS problem `x_hat = (H^T * W * H)^-1 * H^T * 
W * z`. This is equivalent to `x_hat = G^-1 * H^T * W * z`.
2.  **Calculate Residuals:** For each measurement `i`, calculate the residual 
`r_i = z_i - h_i(x_hat)`.
3.  **Calculate Normalized Residuals:** For each measurement `i`, calculate the 
normalized residual `r_i_norm = |r_i| / sqrt(S_ii)`, where `S` is the covariance
matrix of the residuals, `S = R - H * G^-1 * H^T`. `R` is the covariance of the 
measurements, `R = W^-1`. So `S_ii = sigma_i^2 - h_i * G^-1 * h_i^T`.
4.  **Check Threshold:** Find the largest normalized residual. If it's less than
or equal to `residual_threshold` (3.0), stop.
5.  **Remove Measurement:** If the largest residual is above the threshold, 
identify the corresponding measurement. Remove its row from `H`, its value from 
`z`, and its weight from `W`.
6.  **Repeat:** Go back to step 1 with the reduced set of measurements.
