I need to solve a unit commitment problem with hydro, thermal, and storage resources under an emissions cap.

**Understanding the problem:**
- 3 hours with different loads
- Hydro and storage have fixed outputs per hour
- Need to dispatch thermal units (coal and gas) to meet residual load
- Must respect min/max generation limits and emission cap of 200 tons
- Minimize cost while being feasible

**Calculations:**
For each hour, residual load = load - hydro - storage_discharge:
- h1: 120 - 35 - 10 = 75 MW
- h2: 150 - 40 - 20 = 90 MW  
- h3: 130 - 30 - 15 = 85 MW

**Thermal units:**
- Coal: 40-100 MW, $22/MWh, 0.75 ton/MWh
- Gas: 20-80 MW, $38/MWh, 0.35 ton/MWh

Since coal is cheaper, I should use it as much as possible. Let me find the cost-minimal feasible dispatch:

**Hour 1 (75 MW residual):**
- Use coal at min 40 MW, gas for remaining 35 MW
- Cost: 40×22 + 35×38 = 880 + 1330 = $2210
- Emissions: 40×0.75 + 35×0.35 = 30 + 12.25 = 42.25 tons

**Hour 2 (90 MW residual):**
- Use coal at max 100 MW (but need only 90, so coal at 90 MW)
- Cost: 90×22 = $1980
- Emissions: 90×0.75 = 67.5 tons

**Hour 3 (85 MW residual):**
- Use coal at max 100 MW (but need only 85, so coal at 85 MW)
- Cost: 85×22 = $1870
- Emissions: 85×0.75 = 63.75 tons

**Total:**
- Cost: 2210 + 1980 + 1870 = $6060
- Emissions: 42.25 + 67.5 + 63.75 = 173.5 tons (under 200 cap ✓)
Done.
