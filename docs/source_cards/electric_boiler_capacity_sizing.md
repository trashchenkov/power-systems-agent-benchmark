# Source card: electric-boiler capacity sizing

Family `electric_boiler_capacity_sizing` (`REL-TASK-055`) models heat-electrification sizing. The public executable instance computes residual thermal demand after heat-pump/waste-heat contributions, applies a sizing margin and boiler efficiency, rounds to a discrete capacity increment, and reports feeder peak demand. It is grounded in standard building heat-support and demand-electrification planning calculations.
